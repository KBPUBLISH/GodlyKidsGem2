# Player-led community goals

The player chooses one shared goal. Characters do not create competing requests. The Goal button shows the chosen goal; the row beneath it shows the next task and opens the goal's progress. Setting a goal closes the picker without entering placement.

## First five suggestions

1. Welcome a chicken: build a coop, buy a chicken, and discover the coop activity.
2. Grow my garden: till, plant, water, and harvest five squares.
3. Make a cottage: add wood storage if needed, gather 30 wood and 17 stone, then choose a site and build.
4. Build my first kitchen: gather 20 stone and six clay, then choose its site and build. The starter kitchen opens the pantry and cooking activities. Existing ovens count as starter kitchens and retain their cooking progress.
5. Grill a fish: fish at the dock, build a campfire, grill, then eat or share the catch.

Players can choose any of these immediately. They are suggestions rather than compulsory assignments. Completing goals unlocks additional storage, farm and community buildings. Completing garden milestones unlocks harvest goals of 12, 24, 48 and 96 crops in order. Existing progress survives goal switching and loading older cottage/church saves.

## Shared help

Friends use the active goal rather than personal daily assignments. They gather missing wood and stone, tend crops, fish, and help with placed construction. Gathering stops when enough stock is available; energy, access, tools, storage and work limits still apply. The player performs purchases, material crafting and minigames. Helpers never spend resources or choose a building site just because a goal was selected.

## Testing

Choose an oven without materials: only Set as goal appears, the menu closes, and there is no placement ghost. Confirm all friends show the shared goal. Collect the supplies and choose the final build step: placement now opens. Switch to a garden goal: helpers stop gathering building materials and tend the garden instead. Reload and confirm progress. Check larger building goals include enough storage capacity.
